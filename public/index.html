SHIFT DAO LLC Legal Guidelines

Mandatory marketing rules enacted April/May 2026. All copy must follow these strictly.

CRITICAL BANS (ZERO TOLERANCE)
  • No Yield Promises: Never mention "guaranteed returns", "risk-free yield", "earning interest", or "stable gains".
  • No Banned Keywords: Phrases like "moon", "no KYC", "bank-grade", or "profits" are legally blocked.
  • Product vs Entity Distinction: Never say "Shift has an app" or "Shift is an exchange". Refer to the product as Shift Stocks Series Tokens and the issuer as SHIFT DAO LLC (Marshall Islands DAO LLC).
HEURISTIC WARNINGS
  • Asset Wording: Use "backing/reserve assets" instead of "underlying assets". Say "tokenized exposure of X" instead of "tokenized stock X".
  • Regulation Claims: Do not assert custom regulatory approvals or custom compliance credentials beyond the Marshall Islands DAO LLC Act framework.
